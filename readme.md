# Drop and tilt bracket
This is a quick and basic openscad model, it's small and relatively quick to print. All parts are printed.
The arm will rotate from vertical to near horizonal with the top of the bracket remaining inline with the track.
The bracket can rotate in either direction.

## Exploded Diagram
![Exploded diagram](/Images/exploded.png)

## Printing
All parts in the diagram should be printed once.
This should give you 
two pins (red)
one rotating arm (green)
one sliding arm (blue)
one support (yellow)


## Assembly instructions
1. insert one pin from each side of the rotating arm (make sure to insert the pin from the side with the beveled edge)
2. press one of the pins (it doesn't matter which) into the middle hole on the sliding arm
3. sit the large pin of the sliding arm into the track on the support, locate the other pin above the hole at the base of the track and push it firmly home. The arm can be in any position whilst this is done.


## Known Problems
* pinned joints can be stiff to install, I used a small trigger clamp to push them home


The pinned joints provide constraint and the ability to rotate
